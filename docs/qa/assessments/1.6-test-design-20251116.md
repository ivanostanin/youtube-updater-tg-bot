# Test Design: Story 1.6

Date: 2025-11-16  
Designer: Quinn (Test Architect)

## Test Strategy Overview

- Total test scenarios: 10
- Integration/Process tests: 4 (40%)
- Operational validation (cluster/monitoring): 3 (30%)
- End-to-end manual (Telegram): 3 (30%)
- Priority distribution: P0: 6, P1: 4, P2: 0, P3: 0

## Risk Coverage

- **Release provenance gaps:** 1.6-REL-001/002 prevent unsigned or untracked images from reaching production by tying CI artifacts to Helm values.
- **Secret exposure or drift:** 1.6-OPS-001 enforces secret indirection and validates rotation guidance before rollout.
- **Mis-sized PVC or resource exhaustion:** 1.6-REL-004 catches configuration regression via dry-run and live verification.
- **Ingress/TLS outages:** 1.6-OPS-002 confirms certificate readiness and webhook reachability from outside the cluster.
- **User-impacting regressions:** 1.6-E2E-001/002 ensure both private and group subscription flows behave correctly post-deployment.
- **Silent pod failures:** 1.6-OPS-003 guarantees alerting pathways fire on restarts/errors, reducing MTTR.
- **Data loss after restarts:** 1.6-OPS-004 validates persistence and continued notifications through pod churn.

## Recommended Execution Order

1. Run CI-focused checks (1.6-REL-001/002) immediately after tagging to unblock artifact publication.
2. Validate Helm rendering/settings (1.6-REL-003/004) before touching production.
3. Provision and verify secrets plus ingress/TLS (1.6-OPS-001/002) to ensure environment readiness.
4. Execute alerting and persistence operational drills (1.6-OPS-003/004).
5. Finish with high-signal Telegram E2E smokes (1.6-E2E-001/002) once production is upgraded.

## Coverage Gaps & Notes

- Automated evidence capture for Telegram flows is manual; consider scripted bots or recorded sessions for repeatability.
- Cert-manager renewal monitoring is validated indirectly—future work could add synthetic expiry checks.
- Alerting validation relies on existing monitoring stack (Story 1.5); execution needs coordination with on-call to avoid noisy incidents.
- PVC persistence test assumes SQLite; once Story 1.10 migrates to PostgreSQL, scenarios must be updated for multi-instance databases.
